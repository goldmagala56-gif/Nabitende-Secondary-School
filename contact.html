Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school hours?

Classes run from Monday to Friday. Morning preps begin at 6:30 AM, official lessons start at 8:00 AM and end at 4:00 PM. Evening preps for boarders run from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Saturday is reserved for co-curricular activities and remedial classes for candidate classes.

Does the school provide meals for day scholars?

Yes, day scholars can opt into the school feeding program at an additional cost of UGX 150,000 per term. This covers breakfast and lunch on all school days. Alternatively, day scholars may bring packed lunch from home.

What is the school's policy on uniforms?

All students must wear the official Nabitende Secondary School uniform, which consists of a white shirt, green trousers/skirt, green tie, and black shoes. The school sweater is green with gold trim. Uniforms can be purchased from approved vendors near the school or from the school bursar's office.

Are there bursaries available?

Yes. We offer need-based and merit-based bursaries. Need-based bursaries are awarded to students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds, while merit-based bursaries go to top-performing students in each class. Applications open at the beginning of each academic year. Parents must provide supporting documentation such as LC1 letters and proof of income.

How can I check my child's academic progress?

Parents receive printed report cards at the end of every term. Additionally, we hold Parents-Teachers Meetings once per term where you can discuss your child's progress directly with teachers. For urgent concerns, parents may call the Director of Studies or visit the school office during working hours.
