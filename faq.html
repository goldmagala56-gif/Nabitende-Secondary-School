Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what parents and students ask us most.

Students wear cream and sky-blue — cream shirts/blouses with sky-blue trousers or skirts. Full uniform details and where to purchase them are shared upon admission.

Yes, we offer both Day and Boarding options for O-Level and A-Level students. Boarding fees are separate from tuition — see our Fees page for details.

Visit our Admissions page to see requirements and the application process, or contact the school office directly to begin an application.

Parents' visitation days are scheduled once per term — see the Academic Calendar page for confirmed dates.

Transport arrangements vary by area. Please contact the school office to ask about routes serving your location.

Fees can be paid via bank deposit, mobile money, or directly at the bursar's office. Full details are on our Fees Structure page.

We offer a range of Arts and Science combinations at A-Level. Visit our Academics page for the full subject list.

General inquiries can go through our Contact page. For urgent matters, the school office can direct you to the right department.
