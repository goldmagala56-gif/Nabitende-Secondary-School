Academics August 10, 2025

UCE 2025: Nabitende Secures Another 100% Pass Rate

For the fourth consecutive year since our first UCE sitting, Nabitende Secondary School has achieved a 100% pass rate. Out of 112 candidates, 58 scored First Division, 42 scored Second Division, and 12 scored Third Division. The best student, Tumusiime Joel, scored 8 distinctions including perfect grades in Mathematics and Physics.

"This result proves that rural schools can compete with urban institutions when there is commitment from teachers, students, and parents," said Headteacher Mr. Ssemwogerere David during the results celebration.
