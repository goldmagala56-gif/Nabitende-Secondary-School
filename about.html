Our History

Born from community need

Nabitende Secondary School was founded in 2019 by a group of visionary community leaders and education enthusiasts who recognized the urgent need for a quality secondary school in Nabitende Subcounty, Iganga District.

Before our establishment, students from this area had to travel long distances to access secondary education — often walking over 10 kilometers daily. Many dropped out due to the burden of distance and cost. We changed that.

Starting with just two classrooms and 45 students in Senior One, we have grown into a fully-fledged secondary school with over 600 students, modern facilities, and a reputation for UNEB excellence that rivals schools in urban centers.

Who We Are

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable secondary education that empowers young people in Nabitende and surrounding communities with knowledge, skills, and values for national development and global citizenship.

Our Vision

To be the leading rural secondary school in Eastern Uganda, recognized for academic excellence, moral integrity, and producing responsible citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

  • Integrity and Honesty

Our Campus

Located in the heart of Nabitende

Our school sits on a spacious 5-acre campus in Nabitende Subcounty, Iganga District, Eastern Uganda. The serene rural environment provides an ideal atmosphere for focused learning away from urban distractions.

Modern Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms with adequate furniture for all students

Science Laboratory

Fully equipped lab for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology practical lessons

Library & Reading Room

Growing collection of textbooks, reference materials, and past UNEB papers

Sports Grounds

Football pitch, netball court, and athletics track for co-curricular activities

Boarding Facilities

Safe dormitories with matron supervision for students from distant villages
