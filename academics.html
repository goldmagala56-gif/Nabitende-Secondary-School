S1 — S4 | Ordinary Level

O-Level (UCE)

Our Ordinary Level program covers four years (Senior 1 to Senior 4) and prepares students for the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) examinations administered by UNEB. We offer a balanced curriculum that meets the requirements of the Uganda National Examinations Board.

Compulsory Subjects:

English Language Mathematics Biology Physics Chemistry History Geography CRE

Optional Subjects:

Agriculture Commerce Entrepreneurship Fine Art Literature in English Luganda Computer Studies

100% UCE Pass Rate Since 2022

Our first UCE candidates sat in 2022. Every cohort since has achieved a 100% pass rate with the majority scoring First and Second Divisions.

S5 — S6 | Advanced Level

A-Level (UACE)

Our Advanced Level program spans two years (Senior 5 and Senior 6) and prepares students for the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) examinations. Students select subject combinations based on their career aspirations and university requirements.

Subject Combinations Offered:

1
PCB / PCM

Physics, Chemistry, Biology (or Mathematics) + Subsidiary Math/ICT

2
HED / HEL

History, Economics, Geography (or Literature) + Subsidiary Math/ICT

3
Business Combination

Economics, Entrepreneurship, Agriculture (or Geography) + Subsidiary Math

General Paper (GP) — Compulsory

All A-Level students take General Paper to develop critical thinking, communication, and current affairs awareness essential for university success.
